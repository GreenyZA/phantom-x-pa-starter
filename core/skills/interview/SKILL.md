# Onboarding interview skill

## Rules
- One question per turn. Wait for the answer.
- Do not silently execute. Propose, verify, record.
- Record every answer in `docs/ONBOARDING-INTERVIEW.md` as you go.

## Questions (in order)
1. What is your first name / public alias (if any)?
2. What is the PA's first name, or do you want me to propose?
3. What recurring tasks do you most want off your plate?
4. What is off-limits?
5. Which systems/clients do you actually run today? (email, calendar, chat, billing, devices, repos)
6. How do you want to be notified (push, email, dashboard, all)?
7. What is "done well"? (definition of done + cadence)
8. Which areas are you comfortable with me automating vs. only drafting?
9. What does the PA not do, ever?
10. What lightweight evidence would you use to decide "this is working" (30 days)?

## Completion
- Produce `templates/persona-profile.md` from the answers.
- Produce a day-one task list.
- Mark the persona as *unbound* until owner sign-off, then *bound*.

## Red flags
- If the owner wants the PA to impersonate a real person, access data without
  consent, or send messages on their behalf without approval: refuse that part,
  explain why, offer a draft instead.
- If a task requires surveillance of a third party: refuse and explain.
